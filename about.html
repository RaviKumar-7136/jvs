About JVS Agro Mills

JVS Agro Mills is a Hyderabad-based food manufacturing company dedicated to delivering premium-quality wheat flour made from carefully selected grains. Our flagship brand, Grain Pure, is built on the promise of purity, freshness, and consistent quality for every household.

We source high-quality wheat, process it under hygienic conditions using modern milling technology, and package it to preserve freshness and nutrition. Every pack is prepared with a commitment to food safety, honesty, and customer satisfaction.

“At JVS Agro Mills, we believe that healthy families begin with wholesome food. Our mission is to provide pure, nutritious, and affordable flour that brings the goodness of fresh wheat from our mill directly to your home.”
