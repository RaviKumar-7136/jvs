Introduction

Welcome to the JVS Agro Mills website. We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy documents the type of details we collect, how it is secured, and how it will be processed when you browse our site or fill out our dealership and inquiry forms.

Information We Collect

When you contact us using our contact forms or product enquiry triggers, we collect information that can identify you. This includes:

  • Your full name
  • Phone and mobile number
  • Email address
  • Nature of your inquiry or the products of interest (e.g. Grain Pure Chakki Fresh Atta, Maida, or Sooji)
  • Any commercial details or bulk requirements you share in the text areas

How We Use Your Information

The details we collect are solely used to run our business operations and provide customer support services. Specifically:

  • To follow up on your product and dealership inquiries.
  • To coordinate logistics or deliver product updates to you.
  • To analyze user feedback and improve our milling standards or brand packaging.
  • We do not sell, rent, or lease our mailing lists or customer data to third-party marketing companies.

Data Security

We deploy standard security protocols to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, theft, or modifications. While no internet transmission is 100% secure, we guarantee that all offline storage and local databases hosting inquiry records are secured using standard passwords and authorization roles.

Cookies Policy

Our website may use standard cookies to improve page loading speeds, save navigation preferences, and evaluate website traffic trends. You can configure your browser to disable cookies, though some features of our site may not load optimally as a consequence.
